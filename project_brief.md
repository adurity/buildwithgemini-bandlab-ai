# My agent: BandLab AI (Music Band & Album Art Concept Studio)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps musicians and music producers invent band names, build a discography catalog, and produce concept album art and merch designs.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user's musical genres/vibes, preferred aesthetics (e.g., retro-synth, grunge, minimal), existing band name candidates, and previous album ideas.
- Tools: Looks up/validates name availability, fetches genre tropes/themes, adds albums to the band catalog, generates tracklists with durations.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of created bands, albums, tracklists, and merch mockups rendered as rich A2UI cards and track tables.
- Image gen: Generates album cover art, band logos, and vinyl/merch previews matching the chosen visual style.
- Sandbox: Computes album runtimes, track sequencing timings, and royalties/streaming payout estimates.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for runtime/pricing calculations; external audio/lyrics APIs; audio generation.
